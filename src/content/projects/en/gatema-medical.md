An audio and video system for operating theatres. It captures what the cameras
and instruments in the room see and carries it out live, so surgeons,
consultants and students can watch from elsewhere.

I built the live streaming system and its macOS and iOS applications. The
product line continues today as the Artemis system from MEDUTECH.
