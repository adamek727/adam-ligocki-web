**Brno Urban Dataset.** First author. Four cameras, two 3D LiDARs, a thermal
camera, an IMU and centimetre-accurate RTK GNSS on one vehicle, across more than
350 km of recorded driving. Published at IEEE ICRA 2020 and released publicly. A
winter extension followed in Data in Brief.

**Atlas Fusion.** First author. A real-time framework that fuses RGB and thermal
cameras, 3D LiDAR, IMU and GNSS into a single model of what surrounds a vehicle.
Later rebuilt on ROS 2.

**Doctorate and teaching.** Ph.D. in Cybernetics and Artificial Intelligence,
2022, on sensor fusion for 3D dynamic mapping. Eighteen peer-reviewed
publications, including work on detection in thermal and multi-modal images. I
am also the sole author of BPC-PRP, the university's practical robotics and
computer vision course, and taught it until 2026.
