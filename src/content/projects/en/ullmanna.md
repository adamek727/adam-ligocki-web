ULLMANNA builds AI-driven mechanical weeding for organic farming: cameras,
lights and vision software on a farm implement that tells a crop from a weed and
removes the weed inside the row.

I joined in 2023 as an AI specialist and became CTO in 2024, responsible for
computer vision, machine learning and the architecture of the whole control
system. The product worked when I arrived, but every weeding run needed a person
beside the machine and the software crashed constantly. I rebuilt the control
system around that problem rather than around the weed detector.

By 2026 the deployed fleet had grown from ten machines to two hundred, fifty of
them running at the same time, unattended, at customers around the world.
