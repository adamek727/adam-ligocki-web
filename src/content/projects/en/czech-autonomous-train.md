A 1950s locomotive carrying a sensor head that scans the track far ahead. It
detects obstacles and unexpected situations — a car on a level crossing, a
person on the track — and stops at a safe distance. RoboAuto was the main
technology supplier; AŽD Praha presented it in December 2021.

Rail is harder than road in one specific way: a train cannot steer around
anything. Braking is the only option, so detection has to happen far enough
ahead that braking still works.
